About and contact FROID

About and contact

A good conversation starts with context.

FROID brings together two distinct offerings: support for clinical observation and the organization of psychosocial risk management at work. Our presentation separates product, method and limits to support an informed purchasing decision.

Choose the subject

Clinical practice and clinics

Tell us your specialty, whether you practice individually or as a team, and what you would like to evaluate. Do not include patient information.

Companies, HR and OHS

Describe the size of the organization, its units and the project objective. The team can discuss scope, readiness and proposal.

Prepare for the conversation

Data-Froid and research

Ask for information on availability, eligible data, governance and the possibilities for aggregated queries.

Data, security and privacy

Ask to be referred to the person responsible for the subject. Do not send medical records, recordings or third parties' sensitive data through this channel.
