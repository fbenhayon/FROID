FROID ISO 45001

NR-1, ISO 45001 and ISO 45003 in the same management system.

NR-1 gives Brazilian legal force to occupational risk management. ISO 45001 structures the OH&S management system. ISO 45003 gives guidance for psychosocial risks. FROID works in the layer that must be demonstrable: evidence, criteria, traceability and effectiveness verification.

Legal duty and guidance

NR-1A mandatory Brazilian rule that requires work-related psychosocial risk factors to be included in GRO/PGR with identification, assessment, classification, prevention measures, owners, deadlines and follow-up.
ISO 45001The architecture for an occupational health and safety management system. It can be audited and certified, but it does not replace national requirements.
ISO 45003Guidance for managing psychosocial risks within an ISO 45001-based OH&S system. It supports method design, but does not by itself prove NR-1 compliance.

How they connect

Plan

Identify psychosocial factors, exposed groups, evidence sources, grading criteria and cuts that preserve anonymity.

Do

Register prevention measures, owners, deadlines and existing controls without confusing listening with corrective action.

Check

Reassess after the measure is executed to show effectiveness, insufficient evidence or the need for correction.

Act

When the second assessment fails, the system has not failed: it has shown that the cycle must be corrected and documented.

Psychosocial taxonomy

ISO 45003 organizes the subject by sources of risk at work. In Brazil, that reading must also connect with NR-1 and NR-17 when the company uses ergonomic assessment to characterize real work conditions.

Work organizationWorkload, pace, hours, autonomy, role clarity, change management and pressure for targets.
Social factorsRelationships, leadership, recognition, organizational justice, culture, harassment and retaliation.
Environment, equipment and taskPhysical conditions, ergonomics, isolation, remote work, hazardous tasks and insufficient resources.

Governance and dates

The revised Chapter 1.5 of NR-1, introduced by MTE Ordinance 1,419/2024 and postponed by MTE Ordinance 765/2025, took effect on May 26, 2026. The topic moved from voluntary well-being language to documented management evidence.

What must connect: PGR, risk inventory, action plan, PCMSO, support and reporting channels, CIPA, leadership, legal, compliance, HR, OH&S and external providers.

What FROID delivers

NR-17/AEP supportInstrument, cuts and evidence to support psychosocial characterization, without replacing the contractor's field assessment.
InventoryFactors, exposed groups, existing measures, risk classification and indication of evidence sufficiency or insufficiency.
Action planMeasures, owners, deadlines, follow-up and the link between identified risk and planned action.
CriteriaSeverity, probability, matrix, risk levels, publication rules and decision rationale.
HistoryBaseline, executed action, reassessment and record of effectiveness, failure or need for correction.

Limits

FROID does not declare ISO 45001 certification, certify ISO 45003, replace management-system audits or assume the organization's OH&S decisions. It also does not conduct field assessment, implement prevention measures or issue legal opinions. It organizes evidence so management, legal, compliance, HR and OH&S can discuss the same documented cycle.
