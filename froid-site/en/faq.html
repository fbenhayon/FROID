FROID Psique · Frequently asked questions

Answers before the decision.

Understand how to use FROID Psique without confusing an instrumental record with a clinical conclusion.

Measures and interpretation

Does FROID identify seven emotions?

No. It organizes movements into seven families of compatible patterns. The configuration does not determine the person's feelings. Meet the families.

Does confirming a pattern mean emotional certainty?

No. It means the same variant reappeared in distinct frames. Context and professional assessment are still necessary.

Does facial recording work without voice available?

Yes, when the facial capture is valid. Each channel declares its measurement capability independently.

Does the system recognize a fake smile or false calm?

No. Variants are described by the available measures, with no conclusion about authenticity or emotional concealment.

Were the 12 bands replaced by seven families?

No. The bands organize vocal energy; the families organize facial movements. They are different dimensions. See the technology.

Do IPM and IDM measure conflict between voice and face?

No. They are derived from vocal measures. Facial-vocal dissonance is without measurement capability.

Use and subscription

Do I need special equipment?

The proposal uses a camera, microphone and browser. Capture quality, permissions, device compatibility and connection must be verified at implementation; we do not promise adequate reading under any condition.

Does the system record the session for playback?

The architecture described for this version does not provide a replayable audio or video file. Transcript, measures and notes may remain stored in accordance with the applicable documents. See the flows and retention limits.

How do I subscribe?

The V2 model separates analysis credits from the organizational license. See the model and ask the team for the current terms.

Does FROID replace the professional or monitor emergencies?

No. It supports observation and review; it does not replace assessment, diagnosis, clinical conduct or an emergency service. The absence of an alert does not indicate the absence of risk.
