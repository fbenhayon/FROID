Two separate contracts, each with a clear rule: prepaid credit packs for analyses, and a monthly per-clinician license for the organization. Prices in Brazilian reais, fetched live.

The trial applies once per account, after e-mail verification. Buying a pack does not end the trial, and free balance is consumed before paid balance. On expiry, only the remaining free balance expires — purchased credits are never removed.

Analysis credits

FROID PRO packs

Each new care session consumes one analysis credit — once. Reanalysis, reprocessing and report regeneration of the same session are never charged again. The credit is reserved before processing, and consumption is only confirmed once the report is delivered: a technical failure releases the reservation.

For the independent professional, the packs are all you need: buy credits directly and use them in your practice — no organizational license and no monthly fee. The license exists only for clinics with a team.

Fetching current prices…

The pack price is the amount actually charged, in Brazilian reais (BRL), confirmed by Stripe before any credit is granted. The unit price is informational. Purchased credits do not expire.

Organizations

Monthly license per clinician

The organizational license is a contract separate from credits: it covers the clinic structure — roles, supervision, scheduling and governance — and is sized only by active clinicians. Administrative users (front desk, finance, audit) are free and unlimited. Solo professionals need no license: the credit packs above are all they need.

What the license covers — the organization's full clinical package

  • Administrative team at no cost, no limit: front desk, finance and audit never count toward the license — only active clinicians are billed.
  • Cumulative roles with governance: organization administration, role and permission management, and an audit trail of administrative actions.
  • Clinical boundary enforced in the database itself: front desk, finance and audit cannot access records, reports or any clinical content — nor can an administrator without their own clinical enablement.
  • Clinical supervision by explicit assignment: a supervisor reads only the clinicians assigned to them, and revocation cuts access immediately.
  • Organizational scheduling: the front desk books, reschedules and cancels without touching clinical content; double-booking is impossible, every change is kept in the history, and each clinician's availability is respected.
  • Shared credit wallet: the team draws from a single organizational balance, with no per-clinician quota.
  • No-surprise billing: a new clinician is active immediately, with the adjustment accrued to the next invoice; reductions apply at once and never create a mid-cycle charge.

A new clinician is active immediately, with the financial adjustment accrued to the next invoice — never a surprise mid-cycle charge. Reductions apply operationally at once and financially on the next cycle. The license grants no analysis credits.

Access rule

An empty balance only blocks new analyses

Without credits, login still works, history remains, delivered reports remain accessible. Only starting a new billable analysis becomes unavailable — with the reason stated on screen and the purchase path next to it.
