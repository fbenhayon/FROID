Understand how FROID Psique works

A guide to observing a session and reviewing its results. This page captures no signals and presents no patient measurements.

IPM · IDM · Zonas FROID

Zones are a proprietary interpretative framework. Read the index and facial map descriptions; a zone alone establishes neither an emotion nor a diagnosis.

Technology and limits

Read measurements in their session context

No measurement available

Values require an authorized session with signal capture. In the application, check signal availability, baseline and the displayed time window before interpreting IPM or IDM.

From professional report to patient document

End the session, check segments and available transcription, review summaries and record your observations. Select the items intended for the patient and release the document; the patient does not automatically receive the whole clinical workspace.
