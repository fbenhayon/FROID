Explore three situations

A reading repeats: does the record increase?

Repeating the same stored frame does not count as a new observation. The variant needs to reappear in distinct frames.

Facial capture was interrupted

Expired readings no longer represent the present. The earlier event remains in the history; the current condition reports no assessment.

The voice is unavailable, but the face is valid

The facial record can continue. Each channel reports its own capability; the vocal index is not filled in with zero.

What the professional takes to the review

The record preserves the origin and state of the information. The useful question is not “which emotion was proven?”, but “what was observed, under what conditions and in what context of the conversation?”.

Calibration and cuts: the session numbers

Every session begins with 60 seconds of calibration. In that interval the system establishes the mean and variability of the patient's resting fundamental frequency, maps the noise profile of the environment and the microphone, and locks the reference values that will be used in all comparisons of that session.

The session is divided into cuts, and they come from three origins. The automatic one closes every 10 minutes. The manual one is yours, through the button over the video or by Ctrl + Space, and is only accepted from 10 seconds after the previous cut. The closing one closes what remains when the session ends — it exists because, without it, the last minutes were lost.
