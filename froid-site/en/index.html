FROID

Frequency Recognition of Internal Dynamics

Two products. Each in its own context.

Healthcare professionals and clinics

FROID Psique

Vocal measures, records of facial movements and transcription support the review of the session. The observation comes from the system; the clinical meaning is built by the professional.

See the session, step by step

Companies, HR and OSH

FROID NR-1

Organize the assessment of work-related psychosocial factors, the documentation and the follow-up of actions. It is not a clinical assessment of workers.

Learn about the employer process

A support panel, not an autopilot

Just as a dashboard gathers information for whoever is driving, FROID Psique organizes records for whoever is providing care. The panel informs; it does not choose the course of action.

The clinical product presents 12 acoustic bands and the vocal indices IPM and IDM, alongside seven families of facial patterns. They are separate channels: facial movements do not multiply voice indices nor demonstrate conflict between face and speech.

The difference shows up in the record

The present, separate from the history

An expired reading does not go on representing the present. The panel and the report use the same facial interpretation engine.

Absence stated

When there is no condition for ascertainment, the system says so. Unavailable information is not filled in with zero.

Context to investigate

Data-Froid brings together the aggregate analysis proposal, while FROID Explains helps consult available knowledge and measures.

Meet Data-Froid

Artificial intelligence you can understand

A neural network learns patterns from examples. Think of layers that transform an input into useful representations — not of a mind capable of knowing feelings. FROID Explains uses information retrieval to support answers, which remain subject to professional verification.

Trust requires explanation

Know what is measured, what is an estimate and what still needs evaluation. We do not promise automatic diagnosis or the identification of emotions from a facial configuration.
