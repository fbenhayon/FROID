1. Nature of the service

FROID is a tool to support clinical listening. It does not perform automated diagnosis and does not replace assessment, treatment, or judgment by a licensed health professional.

2. Professional responsibility

The use of the indicators shown on the dashboard is the sole responsibility of the professional interpreting them, always alongside the clinical context of the session.

3. Patient consent

It is the professional's responsibility to obtain and record the patient's consent for recording and processing session audio/video.

4. Limitation of liability

FROID is not liable for clinical decisions made based solely on the system's indices.
