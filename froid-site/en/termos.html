Terms of Use

This page is a summary. The official, complete document — with the supplier's full identification, the version code and the SHA-256 digest of the text — is presented inside the application and recorded in the acceptance receipt.

The document that counts

FROID has one document per service, and it is the one your acceptance receipt proves:

  • Terms of Use — FROID Psique — professionals, clinics and attended persons.
  • Terms of Use — FROID NR-1 — company contracting the assessment.
  • Licence Agreement — FROID Psique — professional or clinic that contracts.
  • Services Agreement — FROID NR-1 — contracting company.

This page does not repeat their text. Repeating would create a second version of the same document, and the copy would age in silence — which is exactly what happened to the version code that used to be stamped here.

The essentials

  • FROID is a support instrument, and does not replace the assessment, diagnosis, conduct or records of the qualified professional.
  • FROID does not record the session in audio or video and offers no functionality to preserve it for later playback.
  • Transcription and AI content are auxiliary and may contain errors: the professional must review them before using them in a document or decision.
  • FROID Psique is not an emergency service and the absence of an alert does not mean the absence of risk.
  • FROID NR-1 assesses working conditions, never the worker as a person, and the employer never receives individual answers.
