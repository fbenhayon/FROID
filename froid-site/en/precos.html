FROID Psique / V2 model

Credits to analyze. A license to organize the team.

Two separate components, without confusing analysis consumption with the clinic's structure. The current prices and terms must be presented before the purchase is confirmed.

Ask about commercial terms

What is your scenario?

Independent professional

The model provides for use through analysis credit packs, with no monthly organizational license.

Clinic with a team

In addition to credits, the organizational license is sized by active clinical professionals. Administrative users are not part of that count in the V2 model.

Free trial, no card

Checking the current terms…

The trial applies once per account, after e-mail verification. Buying a pack does not end the trial period, and the free balance is consumed before the paid balance. On expiry, only the remaining free balance expires — purchased credits are never removed.

How credits work

A new care session consumes one analysis credit, once. Reprocessing or regenerating the report of the same session must not create a new charge. The reservation precedes processing; consumption is confirmed when the report is delivered, and a technical failure releases the reservation.

Purchased credits do not expire in the V2 model. Any free trial balance has its own rules and must not be confused with the paid balance. Check the current offer before signing up.

Analysis credit packs

Launch pricing: the amounts shown belong to this initial availability phase and will be adjusted soon. Purchasing now secures the pack at the price in effect at the time of purchase.

Fetching current prices…

The pack price is the amount actually charged, in Brazilian reais (BRL), confirmed by Stripe before any credit is granted. The unit price is informational. Purchased credits do not expire.

What the license organizes

  • Team roles and permissions.
  • Organizational scheduling and governance.
  • Supervision by authorized assignment.
  • Shared credit wallet.

The license does not automatically include analysis credits. The adjustment for active professionals and billing must follow the current contract.

Monthly license per clinical professional

What the license covers

  • Administrative team at no cost and no limit: front desk, finance and audit do not count toward the license — only active clinical professionals are billed.
  • Cumulative roles with governance: organization administration, role and permission management, and an audit trail of administrative actions.
  • Clinical boundary enforced in the database itself: front desk, finance and audit cannot access records, reports or any clinical content — nor can an administrator without their own clinical enablement.
  • Clinical supervision by explicit assignment: a supervisor reads only the professionals assigned to them, and revocation cuts access immediately.
  • Organizational scheduling: the front desk books, reschedules and cancels without touching clinical content; double-booking is impossible, every change is kept in the history, and each clinician's availability is respected.
  • Shared credit wallet: the team draws from a single organizational balance, with no per-professional quota.
  • No-surprise billing: a new clinician is active immediately, with the adjustment accrued to the next invoice; reductions apply at once and never create an extra mid-cycle charge.

A new clinician is active immediately, with the financial adjustment accrued to the next invoice. Reductions apply operationally at once and financially on the next cycle. The license grants no analysis credits.

And when the balance runs out?

The model provides for blocking new billable analyses, while preserving access to the history and to reports already delivered. Commercial terms do not extend administrative users' access to clinical content.
