How credits work

Credit packages, clear rules

Plans work as session credit packages: each session completed consumes one credit. No mandatory monthly fee.

🔁 Auto-recharge

Optional and transparent: depends on explicit consent, triggers only when the balance reaches zero, repurchases the same package, keeps the chosen currency, and uses the value validated by Stripe. If payment fails, a new action from the customer may be required.

💳 Stripe confirms every payment

Stripe is the financial authority of the purchase. FROID only releases credits after confirming the completed payment directly with Stripe — amount, currency, package, and beneficiary organization. The browser returning to FROID is not, by itself, enough to grant access; and a failed payment never releases credits.

🤝 Guided onboarding

We are in a phase of controlled availability: onboarding is guided by the FROID team, who help configure the organization, roles, and first patients. Applicable documents and acceptances are presented based on the country entered at sign-up — commercial availability can be enabled separately in each market.

⏸️ Running out of credit never interrupts care or erases the record

A session that already took place is never refused or discarded over credit: the clinical record is always preserved. If the balance reaches zero, the session is delivered normally and recorded as pending settlement, with a notice on every visit to the dashboard.

Up to 10 sessions may stay open. On reaching that limit, starting new sessions is blocked until it is settled — everything already delivered stays accessible. When the plan is renewed, open sessions are automatically credited to FROID and deducted from the new balance, exactly once each.

Support, not diagnosis: FROID is a technology that supports the professional. Its metrics do not constitute automated clinical diagnosis and must be interpreted by a licensed professional.
