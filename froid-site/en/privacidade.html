This is a template document and must be reviewed by legal counsel/DPO before final publication, in compliance with Brazil's LGPD (Law 13.709/2018) and applicable data protection laws in your jurisdiction.

1. Data collected

Session audio and video (with consent), derived acoustic and facial metrics, professional account data, and billing information.

2. Purpose

Real-time processing to support clinical listening; anonymous aggregation for product improvement.

3. Sharing

Session data is not shared with third parties outside the infrastructure and AI providers strictly necessary for the service to function (e.g., transcription, metric explanation).

4. Data subject rights

Access, correction, and deletion of data upon request, under LGPD and applicable local law.
