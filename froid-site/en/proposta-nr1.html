FROID NR-1 · Proposal

Engage a defined path, not a generic promise.

The proposal must make clear what will be assessed, which documents will be produced and how follow-up will take place. The price must match that scope.

Request a customized proposal

What to define before the price

  • Establishments, activities, sectors and headcount covered.
  • Owners, criteria and sources of evidence.
  • Planned campaigns and disclosure conditions.
  • Documents, formats and revisions included.
  • Follow-up, comparison between cycles and support.
  • Term, implementation, payment conditions and responsibilities.

Stages the proposal may cover

  1. Readiness: review of the structure, support and participation conditions.
  2. Initial assessment: organization of the campaign and of the technical evidence.
  3. Documentation and action: records, owners, deadlines and criteria.
  4. Follow-up: evidence of execution and analysis of the results.
  5. Reassessment: comparison and review of the measures where relevant.

The effective scope is that of the accepted proposal and contract. Do not assume that every activity listed is included in every engagement.

Commercial terms

No steps by band. The price follows headcount continuously — growing from 300 to 301 workers does not change plan and does not reprice the contract.

Companies that do not reach the respondent floor

With realistic participation of 65%, a campaign starts producing a releasable result at around 210 workers on site. That is a participation projection, not a size limit: what the rule requires is a number of answers, not a size of company. A site from 15 workers upwards publishes provided everyone answers — in that range the required sample is already the entire headcount, and a single refusal suspends the inventory. There are two floors: anonymity requires 15 answers, and representativeness requires the sample that speaks for the declared headcount — below 98 people that sample becomes a census, meaning the whole site answering. In that case the correct route is the AEP (preliminary ergonomic assessment), mandatory for every organization with employees and not dependent on any floor.

Unlimited campaigns. Each establishment opens as many assessment campaigns as it needs, in any sector, with no charge per campaign or per sector. The monthly amount is the one in the table above, and measuring again never costs more — the opposite of the incentive of whoever charges per survey.
The proof of effectiveness is included, and that is deliberate. It is the stage that NR-1 demands and that almost nobody delivers. Charging for it separately would create the wrong incentive — measure once, issue the report and close, which is exactly what the rule does not accept.

The contract is the reference

FROID does not automatically assume the organization's responsibility for managing risks, nor does it guarantee compliance merely by being engaged. Confirm deliverables and obligations in the versioned documents.

FROID does not conduct field assessment, does not perform an AET (ergonomic work analysis) and does not implement any measure — neither with its own team nor through third parties. Observation of the real activity, dialogue with workers and the execution of measures belong to the contracting company.
